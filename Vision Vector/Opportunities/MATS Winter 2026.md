# MATS Winter 2026

> **Status:** Application deadline passed  
> **Stage 1 deadline:** September 6, 2026  
> **Programme:** 10–12 week full-time AI safety research fellowship  
> **Main locations:** Berkeley, California, U.S. and London, UK  
> **Official page:** https://www.matsprogram.org/program/winter-2026

## Overview
MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars) is a research fellowship supporting AI safety researchers and field-builders. The Winter 2026 programme includes work across:
- Empirical research
- Theory
- Strategy & Forecasting
- Policy & Governance
- Systems Security
- Founding & Field-Building
- Biosecurity

The programme uses a two-stage process:
1. Apply to one or more broad research tracks.
2. Applicants who progress can apply to specific mentor-led streams.

## Eligibility
Applicants are eligible if they:
- Will be **18 years or older before the programme start date**.
- Are from the U.S. or outside the U.S. — both are eligible.

MATS welcomes:
- All backgrounds and experience levels.
- Applicants without prior AI safety experience.

## Time commitment
- Standard commitment: **40 hours/week** for the approximately 12-week programme.
- Exceptionally strong candidates with significant concurrent responsibilities may be considered for reduced commitments of 20 hours/week on a case-by-case basis.
- J1 visa participants coming to the U.S. cannot participate part-time in person.

## Location and format
- Main research phase: Berkeley, CA and London, UK.
- MATS strongly encourages in-person participation.
- Some flexibility and remote arrangements may be possible depending on circumstances.

## Funding and support
The MATS application page lists:
- **$19,200 stipend**
- **$24,000 compute**
- Free housing and food
- Travel coverage
- J1 visa support where needed

## Application requirements
Stage 1 asks for:
- Background information
- Short responses, including track-specific questions
- Contact details for two references

Important: MATS states that **LLMs may not be used to write any part of the application**, unless a specific work test or form explicitly permits it.

## Timeline shown for Winter 2026
- Stage 1 general application: closed September 6, 2026.
- Stage 2 stream applications: early October.
- Second-stage evaluations/interviews: October–November.
- Selection decisions: late November.
