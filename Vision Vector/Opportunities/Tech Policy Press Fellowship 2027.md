# Tech Policy Press Fellowship — 2027

> **Status:** Applications open  
> **Deadline:** September 24, 2026, 11:59 PM EDT  
> **Duration:** 12 months, beginning January 2027  
> **Format:** Remote, part-time  
> **Official announcement:** https://www.techpolicy.press/call-for-applications-2027-tech-policy-press-fellowship-program/

## Overview
The Tech Policy Press Fellowship supports independent reporting and analysis on urgent issues at the intersection of technology and democracy.

The 2027 cohort is designed for mid-career journalists, researchers, and public policy professionals.

## Eligibility
The fellowship is open to:
- Journalists
- Researchers
- Lawyers
- Public policy professionals
- Other candidates with demonstrated interest or background in tech policy, media, or related fields

Tech Policy Press:
- Encourages applicants from the Global Majority and underrepresented communities in media.
- Accepts international applicants and encourages applications from around the world.
- Welcomes technical contributors if their work aligns with the editorial focus.

## Funding
- **$10,000 stipend**, distributed quarterly.

## Expectations
Fellows are expected to:
- Contribute analysis or reporting at least monthly.
- Attend a mandatory monthly cohort meeting.
- Attend monthly check-ins with the editorial team.
- Meet agreed deadlines.
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest.

## Required application materials
1. CV or resume.
2. Cover letter of no more than two pages explaining what the applicant hopes to accomplish.
3. Links to prior publications.
4. Two professional references.

Published work should be publicly accessible online and clearly attributed to the applicant. Social media posts and unpublished material such as class assignments or dissertations are not accepted as published work.

## Selection timeline
- Deadline: **September 24, 2026, 11:59 PM EDT**
- Applicants may be notified of their status by early December 2026.
- A select pool may be invited for an online interview.
- Fellowship begins January 2027 and lasts 12 months.
