# ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP) — 2027 Intake

> **Status:** Upcoming application window  
> **Application window:** November 1–30, 2026  
> **Study start:** Autumn Semester 2027 / HS27  
> **Official page:** https://ethz.ch/students/en/studies/financial/scholarships/excellencescholarship.html

## Overview
ETH Zurich's Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (ESOP) supports outstanding Master's students with funding, mentorship, and access to the ETH Foundation network.

## Funding
For HS27 onwards, ESOP includes:
- **CHF 13,500 per semester** toward living and study expenses.
- **Full tuition-fee waiver.**
- Support for the regular duration of the Master's programme (typically three or four semesters).

The approximate number of scholarships for the 2027/28 academic year is **around 60**, subject to available funds.

## Eligibility
Applicants should:
- Apply for an eligible ETH Zurich Master's programme (MAS programmes are excluded).
- Have an excellent Bachelor's result, generally interpreted by ETH as **top 10% of the Bachelor's programme / grade A**.
- Apply during the **first Master's application window**.
- Not already be enrolled in a Master's programme at ETH Zurich.
- Not already hold a Master's degree.

## Application deadline
**November 30, 2026, 11:59 AM CET** for international Bachelor's degree holders applying for Autumn Semester 2027.

The application window is:
- **November 1–30, 2026**

## Application requirements
ESOP is submitted through ETH Zurich's **eApply** Master's application.

In addition to standard Master's admission documents, applicants need:
- A self-developed **Master's thesis pre-proposal** following established scientific citation practice.
- If the study programme does not request references, the applicant must name **two professors** as reference persons; they will be asked to submit reference letters.

## Important notes
- The selection process may include a phone or video interview in February.
- Final decisions for the 2027/28 intake are expected by the end of March 2027.
- International applicants must use the first application window; late or incomplete applications cannot be processed.
