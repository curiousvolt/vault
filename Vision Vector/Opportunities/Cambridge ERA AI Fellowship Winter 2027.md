# Cambridge ERA:AI Fellowship — Winter 2027

> **Status:** Applications open  
> **Deadline:** September 13, 2026, 11:59 PM Anywhere on Earth (AoE)  
> **Programme dates:** January 18 – March 26, 2027  
> **Location:** Cambridge, UK  
> **Official page:** https://erafellowship.org/

## Overview
The ERA:AI Fellowship is a 10-week, fully funded research fellowship for people working on technical AI safety, AI governance, and technical AI governance questions related to frontier AI risks.

## Eligibility
ERA states that **anyone can apply**. The formal eligibility requirement is:
- Be **18 or older by the start of the fellowship**.

Additional notes:
- Applicants from diverse backgrounds and career stages are welcome.
- Prior formal research experience is helpful but **not required**.
- Employed professionals can apply, but accepted participants generally need to take leave.
- Students can apply, but accepted participants need to arrange a break from studies.

## Funding and support
ERA provides:
- **£10,000 stipend.**
- Visa support.
- Travel expense coverage.
- Meals during working hours.
- Expert mentorship.
- Dedicated workspace and research support.
- Potential extensions through funded Research Scholar positions for top fellows.

## Application process
1. **Written application** — short essay questions; ERA estimates roughly two hours.
2. **Round 1 interviews** — late September for the top candidates.
3. **Round 2 interviews** — early October; references may be contacted.
4. **Offers and mentor matching** — accepted fellows work with ERA to refine projects and match with mentors.

ERA plans to accept approximately **30–35 fellows** for Winter 2027 across:
- Technical AI Safety
- AI Governance
- Technical AI Governance
