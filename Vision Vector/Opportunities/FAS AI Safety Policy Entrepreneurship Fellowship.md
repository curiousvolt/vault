# FAS AI Safety Policy Entrepreneurship Fellowship

> **Status:** Deadline just passed  
> **Application deadline:** September 7, 2026, 11:59 PM ET  
> **Fellowship period:** September 30, 2026 – February 28, 2027  
> **Official page:** https://fas.org/career/ai-safety-pef/

## Overview
The Federation of American Scientists (FAS) Policy Entrepreneurship Fellowship helps participants move a frontier AI safety policy idea toward real-world implementation.

The fellowship emphasizes policy entrepreneurship rather than research alone: identifying policy windows, building coalitions, engaging decision-makers, and developing actionable policy solutions.

## Eligibility and candidate profile
FAS encourages applications from people of all backgrounds. The fellowship is particularly suited to:
- Early- to mid-career professionals.
- People with limited direct public-policy experience who want to translate technical or subject-matter expertise into policy impact.
- Candidates from technical AI research, academia, think tanks, civil society, industry, law, cybersecurity, national security, and related fields.

Selection is holistic and looks for:
- Understanding of an important frontier AI safety, security, or governance challenge.
- A concrete, implementation-oriented policy idea.
- A credible pathway toward adoption.
- Awareness of relevant institutions, decision-makers, and stakeholders.
- Initiative and interest in translating ideas into real-world impact.

## Time commitment
- Average **5 hours/week** (about 20 hours/month).
- Mandatory programming and twice-monthly group check-ins.
- Monthly individual check-ins with an FAS mentor.

## Fellowship activities
Fellows are expected to:
- Develop and publish a policy memo.
- Participate in policy entrepreneurship training and reading groups.
- Attend an in-person California retreat, tentatively November 4–7, 2026.
- Attend an in-person Washington, DC capstone event during the week of February 22, 2027.

## Funding and benefits
- **$5,000 stipend.**
- Potential additional **$1,000 merit award** for the most specific and actionable policy memo and advocacy plan.
- Additional travel stipend for select retreat and capstone expenses.
- Professional editing, mentorship, policy-engagement training, and access to FAS networks and programming.
