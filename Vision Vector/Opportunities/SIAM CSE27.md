# SIAM Conference on Computational Science and Engineering (CSE27)

> **Status:** Upcoming  
> **Event dates:** February 22–26, 2027  
> **Location:** Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, U.S.  
> **Official page:** https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/cse27/

## Overview
CSE27 is SIAM's Conference on Computational Science and Engineering, focused on computational methods, scientific computing, modeling, simulation, and related applications. It will be held jointly with ACDA27 and IMR27.

## Key deadlines
- **Travel support application:** November 30, 2026
- **Early registration:** January 29, 2027
- **Hotel reservation:** January 29, 2027
- **Contributed lecture/poster/minisymposterium/minisymposium abstract deadline:** August 25, 2026 — **passed**
- **Minitutorial proposal deadline:** June 29, 2026 — **passed**

## Travel support eligibility
SIAM travel and registration support is aimed at:
- Full-time students in good standing.
- U.S.-based early-career researchers within six years of receiving their degree.
- For CSE27, SIAM specifically lists full-time students in good standing and U.S.-based early-career researchers who received their PhD after January 1, 2021.

Selection is competitive and considers research relevance, scholarly record, active participation/presentation, ties to SIAM, and recommendations.

## Participation notes
- The conference is fully in person.
- Remote and pre-recorded presentations are not permitted.
- Presenters are expected to register and attend.
