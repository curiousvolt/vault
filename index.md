## About

I’m **Aman Kumar**, an electrical engineer — or so I’m told. That’s the “Volt.” The “Curious” part is me poking my nose into everything else, experimenting, breaking things, and learning along the way.

This is a knowledge base of what I’ve picked up from doing, not just academics: notes from things I’ve learned, documentation I’ve written for myself, ideas I’m exploring, and questions I haven’t figured out yet. It’s a work in progress — part reference library, part digital garden, and part record of things I’ve built and discovered.
