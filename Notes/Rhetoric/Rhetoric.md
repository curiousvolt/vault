# Rhetoric
a way of speaking or writing that is intended to impress or influence people but is not always sincere
> James Engell's on-campus course, "Elements of Rhetoric."

### Rehtoric (3 types)
1. Judicial rhetoric means discussion of a past action to evaluate whether or not it was just
2. Deliberative rhetoric refers to rhetoric intended to persuade.
3. Epideictic rhetoric is used to either celebrate or blame and individual - eulogies (will )
