# Rhetoric
a way of speaking or writing that is intended to impress or influence people but is not always sincere
> [James Engell's](https://jengell.scholars.harvard.edu/) on-campus course, "Elements of Rhetoric."
[[RhetoricMap]]
### Rhetoric (3 types)
1. Judicial rhetoric means discussion of a past action to evaluate whether or not it was just
2. ==Deliberative rhetoric== refers to rhetoric intended to persuade.
3. Epideictic rhetoric is used to either celebrate or blame and individual - eulogies (will fall into this category)


###### Grading
1. A short article (300-600 words) expressing the learner's support or opposition to a proposal, idea, or policy. (self - graded)
2. A short speech (no more than 5 minutes) expressing the learner's support or opposition to a proposal, idea, or policy. (peer graded)
